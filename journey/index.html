How a bottle gets to you

From vineyard
to cellar to table

01

Grown on the slope

Vines ripen through the summer on hillsides picked for sun and drainage. The soil and the slope shape what ends up in your glass.

02

Picked by hand

At harvest the bunches are cut by hand and sorted, so only ripe fruit goes to the press. Then comes fermentation, where the juice becomes wine.

03

Rested in the cellar

The wine ages in oak barrels or tanks for months or years. Reds usually rest longer than whites, which is where depth and softness come from.

04

Poured at your table

We pack each bottle upright and ship it within 24 hours. You open it at home, with the right dish beside it.
