Questions

Who can order?

Anyone aged 21 or over. Age is checked at delivery.

What does delivery cost?

Free when your order is over ₹5,000, otherwise ₹250. Orders ship within 24 hours.

How do I pay?

On delivery, by cash, UPI or card.

How does the three-bottle deal work?

Add any three bottles to your cart and ten percent comes off automatically. You can also pick your three.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Choose Track an Order and enter your order number and phone number.
